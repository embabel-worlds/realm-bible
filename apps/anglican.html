        How it works

        The data

        • The Prayer Book — the Episcopal Church's Book of Common Prayer (1979), which the Church has never copyrighted: the Daily Office Lectionary, the collects in Rite One and Rite Two, the canticles, the Psalter, the Prayers and Thanksgivings, An Outline of the Faith, and the Articles of Religion from its Historical Documents. Read from bcponline.org and stored in the realm.
        • The church year — every day of 2025–2030 computed when the realm was built, by the Prayer Book's own rules: Advent from the Sunday nearest St Andrew, Easter and all that hangs on it, the numbered Propers after Pentecost by the Sunday closest to their date, the two-year lectionary (Year One begins at Advent before an odd year), Holy Days transferred off Sundays and out of Holy Week and Easter Week, and the Eves of feasts. Each morning and evening is one stored row.
        • Colours — the Prayer Book appoints none. The colour shown is custom: blue in Advent (violet is the older use), white at Christmas and Easter, green after Epiphany and Pentecost, violet in Lent, red for Holy Week, Pentecost and martyrs.
        • The Church Catechism — the catechism of 1662, in the American form printed in the 1928 Prayer Book. The English 1662 book is Crown copyright in the United Kingdom, so none of its texts are carried here.
        • Proofs of the Articles — the Scripture a public-domain Catechism of the Thirty-Nine Articles (1877) cites under each Article. Only its references are used.
        • The Bible — the King James and World English Bible, as in the general edition. The Apocrypha is not in it, so a lesson from Ecclesiasticus or Wisdom is named but cannot be opened.

        The questions

        DailyOffice {date, office} — the day, the psalms and lessons, the collect in both rites and the suggested canticles:

        MATCH (o:OfficeDay {date: $date, office: $office})-[:COLLECT]->(c:Collect)
OPTIONAL MATCH (o)-[:CANTICLE]->(k:Canticle)
WITH o, c, collect({key: k.key, number: k.number, name: k.name, …}) AS canticles
RETURN o.name AS day, o.feast, o.season, o.colour, o.psalms, o.lessons, …,
       c.rite1 AS collectRiteOne, c.rite2 AS collectRiteTwo, canticles

        PsalterPsalm {number}, Canticles {name}, ArticlesOfReligion {number}, ArticlesSearch {term}, OutlineOfTheFaith {section}, CatechismLesson, CatechismSearch (the same view the Catholic edition uses, across every catechism the realm holds) and PrayerBookPrayers {name} — each a saved query in the realm, called by name.

        The search and the reader below are the general edition's, shared (bible-core.js): an agentic search over the World English chapters, the reader in KJV or WEB, the people in a chapter, and one verse in seven translations.

        When it looks wrong

        • A different reading from your parish. The Prayer Book lets the officiant re-order readings around a feast and choose among the psalms; this shows what the tables appoint. Lesser feasts are listed but their own readings (in Lesser Feasts and Fasts, which is copyright) are not.
        • Nothing for a day. Only 2025–2030 are stored.
        • A lesson that will not open. It is from the Apocrypha, which this Bible does not hold.

        What made this possible

        • The office, the Prayer Book's texts and the Bible's own chapters are one graph: an office's psalms and lessons are relationships to the chapters, and an Article's proofs are relationships to the chapters it is proved by.
        • This page has no backend of its own. It asks the world for saved views by name.
