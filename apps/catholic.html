How it works

The data

  • The calendar — the General Roman Calendar for 2025–2030, computed with the open-source romcal library (MIT) and stored in the realm: every day's celebrations with rank, liturgical colour and season, holy days of obligation, and movable feasts reckoned from Easter. Local calendars (a country's or a diocese's own feasts) are not included.
  • Saints — each saint the calendar names is matched to Wikidata, the free knowledge base (CC0): dates, places with coordinates, image, religious order, canonization, patronages. Fetched live and cached for a week.
  • Lives — the opening of the saint's English Wikipedia article (CC BY-SA 4.0), fetched live and shown with its link.
  • The Rosary — the four sets of mysteries, their traditional weekdays and their Scripture, stored in the realm.
  • Catechisms — the Baltimore Catechism No. 2 (1891, public domain), questions and answers by lesson. The Catechism of the Catholic Church is copyrighted, so it is not reproduced: topics link to its paragraphs on vatican.va.
  • Prayers — traditional English texts in the public domain.
  • The Bible — the King James and World English Bible, as in the general edition; the Douay-Rheims is one of the seven translations a verse can be compared in.

The questions

TodayInTheChurch {date} — the day's celebrations, rank, colour, season, and the saints each one honours:

MATCH (d:LiturgicalDay {date: $date})<-[:ON]-(c:Celebration)
OPTIONAL MATCH (s:CalendarSaint) WHERE s.saintId IN c.saints
…  RETURN d.date, d.season, d.colour, d.holyDayOfObligation, collect({…, saints}) AS celebrations

SaintLife {wd} — a saint's card from Wikidata and the opening of their Wikipedia article, both fetched live:

MATCH (k:SaintCard {wd: $wd})
OPTIONAL MATCH (k)-[:LIFE]->(l:SaintLife)
RETURN k.wdLabel, k.birthPlace, k.birthCoord, k.deathPlace, k.deathCoord, …, l.extract, l.pageUrl

RosaryToday {date}, CatechismOnTopic {topic}, CatechismSearch {term}, CatechismLesson {catechism, lesson}, SaintsOfCountry {country}, SaintsInScripture, Prayers — each a saved query in the realm, called by name. The catechism panel picks its topic from the day: a Marian feast asks about Mary, the Guardian Angels about angels, any saint's day about the communion of saints, otherwise the season.

The search and the reader below are the general edition's, shared (bible-core.js): an agentic search over the World English chapters, the reader in KJV or WEB, the people in a chapter, and one verse in seven translations.

When it looks wrong

  • No saint on a day. Weekdays in a season, and Sundays, honour no saint; and a day outside 2025–2030 is not in the stored calendar.
  • The life or the map does not load. Wikidata and Wikipedia are public services and sometimes slow or unreachable; the panel says so and links to the saint's Wikidata page instead.
  • A feast your parish keeps is missing. Only the General Roman Calendar is here, not national or diocesan calendars.
  • A saint in the wrong country. Saints are matched to a country by Wikidata's citizenship and birthplace, which reflect today's borders.

What made this possible

  • A stored calendar, a live knowledge base, a live encyclopedia and the Bible's own text meet in one query language: a saint's card is a join to Wikidata made when you ask, and their chapters are a join to the people Theographic tags in each verse.
  • This page has no backend of its own. It asks the world for saved views by name.
